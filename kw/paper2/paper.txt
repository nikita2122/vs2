                     A Deep Network for Threshing Efficiency
                                   and Mechanical Stability

                     A short computer-science paper on the command model
                                of a thesis on thresher efficiency


                                                   Abstract

       A numerical optimizer can raise grain recovery on a thresher if it is given the true feed, the true
       moisture, and a free solve. An onboard program gets a noisy sensor window and a deadline.
       This paper trains a feedforward network to imitate that optimizer. The inputs are the window
       features of a four-station placement. The outputs are four normalized setpoints: intensity,
       residence, cleaning, and feed admission. The teacher maximizes recovery with explicit prices
       on energy, impurity, breakage, and a stability floor at 0.70. On a weighted seasonal mix the
       network lowers grain loss from 2.85% at a fixed setting and 2.50% on a moisture card to
       1.80%, against 1.78% for the teacher. Specific energy rises from 1.41 to 1.63 kWh t−1 . A linear
       imitator on the same features reaches 1.88%. Depth is a small gain on the command and a
       large gain on the companion status task. A rate-limited transient holds the stability floor while
       the feed crests. The study is a numerical experiment, not a field trial.



1     Introduction

Published combine controllers move one or two actuators from a moisture signal, a loss sensor,
or a short predictive model [1, 2, 3]. A moisture card is the simplest of these policies, and it is
not foolish: wetter grain is given a harder and a longer pass. It cannot see a product of feed and
moisture, and it cannot see an axial load that is heavy only at the inlet. An optimizer that sees
both will spend residence on a wet, heavy crop and will refuse that spend on a light crop, because
breakage and energy are in the cost. The optimizer is too slow, and too dependent on the true
disturbance, to be the program that runs every control step [4, 5].
    This paper is the command half of a computer-science thesis. A companion paper places the
sensors and identifies a discrete operating status. The command network does not read that label.
It reads the same features and emits a command. Imitation is used because a teacher is available
from the simulator. A policy search without a teacher would be a different algorithm.


2     Teacher and network

The plant is static and reduced-order. Admitted feed is qe = q(0.82 + 0.18 uF ), so the feed

over the unit box, from a four-level grid and a bound-constrained polish [4]. Here e is specific
shaft energy, ι impurity, b a breakage index that grows with residence and intensity, CV the
coefficient of variation of eight station loads, and S ∈ [0, 1] a stability index. The stability term
is a hinge. Above 0.70 the teacher may spend calmness it does not need. The fixed baseline is
(uI , uR , uC , uF ) = (0.50, 0.44, 0.46, 1). The card raises the first three components with moisture
and leaves the trim open. It does not read q.
    Features are the 23-dimensional window statistics of stations 1, 4, 5, 8 plus torque, vibration,
a loss proxy, return flow, power, moisture, and feed. The network is 23–64–64–32–4, ReLU
hidden units, linear output clipped to [0, 1], 7908 parameters, 7744 multiply-adds [7, 8]. Training
is squared error against the teacher’s command, Adam at learning rate 10−3 , 140 epochs, best
validation epoch 122 [9]. Each disturbance contributes three windows, drawn under the card, under
a random command, and under the fixed setting, all labeled with the same teacher command. One
mixture is the whole adaptation to the deployment distribution [6]. The linear ablation is least
squares on the same standardized features.
    The onboard step standardizes the window, evaluates the net, and limits each component’s
change to 0.08 per 0.2 s. If the companion status net is below confidence 0.55, the step writes the
card instead. The seasonal table does not use that gate. It is the network with a full window.


3     Seasonal evaluation

Eight steady points, wheat-heavy and with rice and soybean included, are weighted to one. Table 1
is the mean. The network is within 0.03 points of the teacher’s loss and within a few hundredths
of the teacher’s energy, impurity, and breakage. The linear imitator recovers most of the gain and
leaves 0.08 points more loss. The card beats the fixed setting by 0.35 points and is not a dummy
baseline.
    The mean hides two rows that decide whether the imitator understood J. On dry, heavy wheat
the card is worse than the fixed setting, because low moisture eases the card while the feed stays
heavy: loss 2.85% against 2.65%. The teacher moves residence up to 0.59 and holds intensity
at 0.50, with loss 1.96% and breakage 1.27%. The network overshoots, writes residence 0.71 and
intensity 0.59, and records loss 1.57% with breakage 1.63%. A loss-only ranking would put the

windows still fall just under the floor. The executive does not project the command back onto
the hinge. That projection would be the teacher, called at runtime.


4     A feed transient

Wheat at moisture 0.17 is held at 5 kg s−1 except for a crest of about 8.2 kg s−1 at t = 12 s.
The network starts on the card and is rate-limited. Figure 1 is the peak load. At the crest the
fixed command reaches 1367 N and 3.19% loss. The network reaches 1118 N and 1.81% loss, with
residence 0.84 and stability still at 0.70. Mean loss over 26 s is 2.59%, 2.38%, and 1.48% for the
fixed command, the card, and the network. At t = 0 the network has not moved, which is the
check that the crest result is not a warm start from the teacher.


5     Conclusion

The command this plant requires is an imitation of a priced optimizer, not a second moisture
card. A network of a few thousand parameters matches the teacher’s seasonal loss and copies the
teacher’s prices, including the prices that make a low-loss overshoot on dry, heavy wheat a worse
command. A linear map on the same features is close. The depth that clearly changes a score in
the parent thesis is the status classifier, not this command head. The transient shows that a rate
limit of the size an actuator can follow is enough for a crest a few seconds wide. Nothing in the
